### Backstory Elements to Include

Craft a backstory that ties you to any continent on Toril but leaves room for growth and mystery on Eldrath. If you would liked to be from Eldrath, that will can work to. Just let me know and we can figure it out together. 
Focus on 2–3 key elements:

1. **Origin & Homeland** – Where on Toril are you from? How does your culture view magic, nature, or the sea? Look at the [Toril world map](https://fcchurchofgod.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Toril_Map.jpg), feel free to look up things about the different lands, or just make something completely up.
2. **Personal Motivation** – What drove you to sail east? (Ambition, redemption, curiosity, duty, escape, home?)
3. **A Secret or Mystery** – Something you haven’t told anyone: a lost memory, a family curse, a prophetic dream of mists and glowing stones, or a debt owed to a mysterious patron. Add at least one, but if you want to add more that is OK as well.
4. **Connection to Nature or Magic** – Optional but encouraged: a childhood bond with the wild, a latent gift you’ve never fully understood, or a traumatic encounter with corrupted magic. Be creative, if you need help ask. 

Try to make a decent backstory with approx 2-3 paragraphs about your character. I like to try to use some of this information in the campaign. Be descriptive, be creative. At the end of the day, have fun with it. If you are struggling to come up with good ideas, I will do my best to help out.

### Bonds, Ideals, & Flaws

Choose or roll traits that tie into the campaign’s themes. Here are some suggestions if you need help deciding:

**Ideals**:
- Balance: Nature must remain in equilibrium; disrupting it invites ruin for all.
- Growth: True strength comes from understanding and mastering oneself.
- Harmony: The elements, like people, thrive when they work in unison rather than conflict.
- Discovery: Hidden truths and forgotten powers are worth any risk to uncover.
- Sacrifice: The greatest good sometimes demands giving up what we hold most dear.
- Destiny: Some paths are written in the stars (or the mists); I will walk mine with open eyes.
- Redemption: Past mistakes can be atoned for through deeds that heal rather than destroy.
- Unity: No one survives alone; true power lies in the bonds we forge with others.
- Preservation: Ancient wonders and wild places must be protected from corruption and greed.
- Transformation: Change is inevitable and often painful, but it leads to greater things.
- Truth: Illusions and lies crumble in time; only raw honesty endures.
- Ascension: Mortals can rise to meet the divine if they prove worthy through trials.

**Bonds**:
- I seek an ancient artifact whispered of in my homeland’s legends
- A vision or prophecy drew me across the sea — I must learn its meaning.
- I owe a life debt to someone lost in the shipwreck (or believe I do).
- My family’s honor depends on uncovering lost knowledge.
- I carry a fragment of an ancient map that points to a glowing stone said to hold the island’s heart.
- A recurring dream of swirling mists and distant songs has haunted me since childhood — I sailed east to find its source.
- I owe my life to a mysterious herbalist or sage who saved me once; their final words urged me toward the southeastern seas.
- My family’s honor was tarnished by a long-ago betrayal; I seek a lost relic to restore our name.
- A prophecy spoken by a dying seer claims I (or someone like me) will either save or doom the enchanted isle.
- I fled my homeland after awakening strange powers I couldn’t control; the sea was my only escape.
- A loved one vanished years ago on a voyage east — I believe they still live somewhere in the mists.
- I am bound by an oath to protect the natural world from unnatural blight and shadow.
- A shadowy patron promised me power in exchange for retrieving something from beyond the horizon.
- The ship I sailed on carried a priceless cargo — its loss is my responsibility to reclaim or atone for.
- Visions of wings and storms have called to me; I must discover if they are gift or curse.
- I carry a small, warm stone that pulses faintly — it began glowing brighter the closer we sailed to Eldrath.

**Flaws**:

- I hide a dark impulse or rage that surfaces in moments of stress.
- I fear losing control of the power I sense awakening within me.
- I hide a deep well of rage that surfaces when I feel nature being harmed or corrupted.
- I distrust anything civilized — cities, laws, even other people — and prefer the wild unpredictability of the island.
- Visions and whispers plague me; I fear they’re signs of madness rather than destiny.
- I crave power above all else and may sacrifice too much (or others) to claim it.
- I am haunted by guilt over a past failure that cost lives; I hesitate when others are at risk.
- I fear losing control of the strange energies awakening inside me.
- I am reckless when pursuing secrets or prophecies, ignoring danger to myself or the group.
- I cling too tightly to old mainland ways and resist the island’s call to change.
- I see enemies and corruption everywhere — even allies can seem suspect in my paranoia.
- I am drawn to fire, storms, or shadow in ways that feel dangerous and addictive.
- I sacrifice too readily for others, risking my own life without hesitation.
- I cannot abide deception; lies (even kind ones) trigger uncontrollable anger or withdrawal.
